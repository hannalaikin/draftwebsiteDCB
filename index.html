Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About the Project

Will local power bills go up?
The presentation states that DC BLOX pays for the power it uses and covers incremental direct interconnection costs associated with the project. :contentReference[oaicite:28]{index=28}
Will these data centers impact water resources?
The uploaded deck explains that the first phase uses a DX waterless cooling system, and larger buildings may use hybrid air and closed loop water systems that recirculate water after the initial priming. :contentReference[oaicite:29]{index=29}
Are there health or environmental risks?
The presentation states that the facilities use no hazardous materials and dispose of no environmental waste, and that backup generators are used only when utility power is unavailable or during testing. :contentReference[oaicite:30]{index=30}
How does this help local residents?
According to the deck, benefits include tax revenue, local labor and job expansion, and improved connectivity through new local fiber networks. :contentReference[oaicite:31]{index=31}
